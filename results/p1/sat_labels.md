# Satellite erosion labels per reach unit (H3 label source b)

Generated by `python src/sat_labels.py` (no reported-inventory data read). Retreat = + (bank moved landward).
In scope: 6465 bank points, 390 units of 2 km, 172 of 5 km; 343 2 km units and 154 5 km units have >= 5 points with a window retreat.

## Definitions

- Point window retreat = mean(dist 2022-2024) - mean(dist 2013-2015), i.e. 2014 -> 2023 (9 yr), each mean needing >= 2 valid years. Pre-window = mean(2012-2014) - mean(2000-2002) (2001 -> 2013, 12 yr).
- Long-term: Theil-Sen lrr 2000-2024 per point (all kinds); sh_nonlinear = share of step or non-linear series.
- Annual: year-to-year change of a 3-yr centred rolling mean (2-yr where 2012 is missing and at 2024, divided by the shift of the mean year); unit-year p90 over points; events p90 >= 10, 15, 20 m/yr.
- Points whose window or pre-window change exceeds 30 m/yr (edge switches: bar attached or detached, island-ring jumps; the fastest validated hotspot is ~22 m/yr) are left out and counted in n_win_jump / n_pre_jump (27 / 127 points); annual changes > 100 m/yr likewise.
- A unit label is left empty when fewer than 5 points have a window retreat (47 2 km units).

Many large positives are step series, not non-linear ones: a riverward jump of 0.5-1.1 km in 2002-2006 (an island or bar joined the bank, so the measured bank moved to its outer edge), then steady retreat of that new bank through the window (e.g. tien_L_+008 T199L, isl_tien_01_I_+003 T66I, isl_tien_02_O_+006 T127I; `results/p1/sat_labels_series_check.png`). Their long-term lrr is negative because of the old step, but the window retreat is real retreat of the present bank. Non-linear positives (tien_L_+015) are accelerating-then-slowing retreat, with one landward step 2011->2013 at T321L just before the window. A true artefact is flip-flopping between two edges (hau_L_+040 T814L, ~70 m alternations; _lin drops the unit, _clean keeps it at the threshold) and a bar that sits in front of the bank and then goes (isl_hau_02_I_+003: bank ~130 m riverward 2015-2021, back in 2022; _clean drops it). tien_R_+059 has a 60-130 m landward jump in 2023 that persists in 2024 (a collapse or a detached bar; check imagery). Primary label therefore uses all points; the _clean variant is the robustness check.

## Noise floor

Per-composite noise sigma = 3.6 m (measured repeatability) or 4.7 m (median robust residual scale of linear series, includes real year-to-year bank wobble). Difference of two 3-yr means: SD = sigma x sqrt(2/3); 95% (1.96 SD) floor 5.8 / 7.5 m with 3 valid years in each mean, 7.1 / 9.2 m with 2. Annual rolling-mean change: 3.3 / 4.3 m/yr. Bank-position bias vs hand clicks (-10 to -20 m) is systematic and largely cancels in a difference. A 20 m point threshold is >= 2x the larger floor; a unit p75 over ~20 points is less noisy still, so the labels are limited by real change, not noise.

## Recommendation

- Primary: **p75_20** (p75 of window retreat >= 20 m): a quarter of the unit (~500 m of a 2 km bank) retreated about 2 m/yr or more over the window, well above the noise floor; robust to a few bad points (multi-edge banks, transient objects) unlike max or p90; sh20_25 is nearly the same rule (p75 >= 20 m <=> at least a quarter of points >= 20 m, up to quantile interpolation), so it is not a separate variant.
- Sensitivity: **p75_30** (stricter, ~3x the larger 95% floor, fewer and more certain positives) and **p90_30** (localised hotspots: ~200 m of bank retreating >= 30 m), plus the 5 km units and p75_20_clean (edge switches inside the window removed).
- All points, not linear + step only: dropping non-linear series changes 3 positives, and dropping by kind is the wrong filter anyway (step and non-linear kinds describe 2000-2024, mostly before the window); the within-window jump filter targets the actual failure. Fixed parameters: MAX_RATE 30 m/yr, WIN_JUMP 60 m, MIN_PTS 5.
- Event table (p90 >= 15 m/yr) for year-matched comparisons; long-term lrr and pre-window retreat as predictors, not labels.

## Caveats

- Units with few points (47 of 390 2 km units have < 5): unit ends cut by the scope or border, island rings with patchy detection; their label is empty, not 0.
- Island rings: side I is the island bank, O the opposite bank of the side channel; channels narrower than the canal filter give non-detections; ring banks within 150 m of a main bank were dropped (no double count).
- Embanked reaches: a bank that retreated before an embankment and then stopped scores low in the window; Landsat sees retreat, not damage or risk. Accretion (negative retreat) is kept as measured.
- 30 m pixels: collapses smaller than ~1 pixel and short-lived scars repaired within a year are missed.
- Positives are concentrated: on the Hau main channel the primary label is almost never 1 (window retreat is small there), so H3 rests mainly on the Tien and the island channels; report H3 by river / channel type.
- Edge switches below 30 m/yr (multi-edge banks, ~100 m) are not caught by the cap; unit p75 limits their effect but a unit made mostly of such points can be mislabelled (check positives on imagery).
- Thresholds chosen from noise and prevalence only, before any comparison with reported sites.
