## 5. Plain-language summary (exploratory)

- 28% [12%, 44%] of the 2 km bank units that measurably eroded in 2014→2023 (13 of 47) never appear in the public reports collected here; 60% [47%, 74%] never appear as a dated event (hamlet-level report or emergency decision). All 13 unreported eroding units are on the Tiền (7 island channels, 6 Tiền main; list in section 1).
- 77% [70%, 84%] of reported bank length (203 of 265 units) shows no retreat above the 9.3 m noise floor over 2014→2023 (warned-reach lists 79% [71%, 88%], news reports 70% [59%, 81%]).
- Reports do not mark where measured retreat events (p90 >= 15 m/yr) happen next: reported units had 0.3x [0.1, 1.0] the calendar-matched event rate of never-reported units in the three years after the report (0.4x [0.1, 1.5] when also matched on channel group) and 0.3x [0.1, 0.8] (0.4x [0.2, 1.1]) in the three years before; units on a warned list had 0.5x [0.0, 1.0] (0.5x [0.1, 1.0]) the event rate of not-warned units in the following 1-3 years.

Caveats: reports locate erosion at commune level (a warned list or a commune-level row labels every bank of the commune), so "no measurable retreat" over a whole reported unit can hide a short failure; 30 m Landsat banks miss collapses smaller than a pixel and scars repaired within a year; embanked banks show no retreat because they were protected (a report followed by an embankment reads as commission); reports cover 2015-2024 only and the satellite window is 2014→2023; the inventory is a search of public news and lists, not a complete official register, so omission here is omission from the public record collected, not from government knowledge. Satellite labels themselves contain a few edge-switch artefacts (see the clean-variant column). Ring banks of adjacent islands are partly measured twice (last column of the list: isl_tien_02_I_+008/+009 and isl_tien_03_O_+006/+007 are largely the same bank at Long Khánh A), so the 13 omitted units are about 11 distinct 2 km banks; the same duplication affects other island units upstream of this analysis (reach_units.py drops ring banks near main banks, not near other rings).
