# Gap scan beyond H1-H3 (2026-10-26)

Purpose: find additional research gaps that (a) were not found in the literature searched, (b) can be answered with
data the project already has, and (c) would strengthen a Q1 submission (NHESS; Geomorphology, IJAEOG fallbacks).
Context: H3 came out NOT supported (`results/p2/h3.md`); H1/H2 forecasts were scored once on the 2019-2024 test period on 26/10 (`results/p2/forecast.md`, `protocol-v1`), so any
new forecast analysis below is post hoc.

## Search scope

- Date: 2026-10-26. Sources: OpenAlex `/works?search=` (84 queries, title/abstract/full-text relevance search,
  15 top hits read per query, abstracts from `abstract_inverted_index`) and arXiv API `export.arxiv.org/api/query`
  (20 queries, ≤ 1 request / 3 s), plus 18 OpenAlex DOI look-ups for full abstracts. No email address was sent anywhere.
  Raw responses are cached in the session scratchpad (`litscan/`), not in the repo.
- Not searched: Google Scholar, Scopus, Web of Science, Vietnamese journals beyond what OpenAlex indexes, cited-by lists.
- arXiv covers this field poorly (e.g. `abs:Mekong` returns 10 records in total; `abs:"river migration"` returns 0),
  so arXiv "not found" carries little weight; OpenAlex is the main source.
- Hit counts are OpenAlex/arXiv totals for the query string. OpenAlex relevance search is broad (large counts are
  mostly noise); the judgement rests on the nearest 5-15 hits that were read, not on the counts.
- Every "not found" below means: not found in the OpenAlex/arXiv searches listed in the query table on 2026-10-26.

## Competitor check for H1-H3 (same searches)

- H1/H2: no application of conformal prediction to bank, shoreline or channel-migration forecasts was found in
  OpenAlex (`"conformal prediction" AND (shoreline OR riverbank ...)`, 21 hits, none relevant) or arXiv (17 hits for
  conformal + shoreline/riverbank/erosion, all conformal field theory or unrelated). New method precedent to cite

  (conformal on river-network graphs; not bank retreat). Singh et al. 2024 (10.1038/s41598-024-65954-w) remains the EO anchor.

  yearly river positions and migration rates from Landsat). Abstract only; check before writing data-availability claims.

## Recommendation

Ranked top 3 (all strengthen the existing paper rather than start a new one):

1. **C1 + C7: what horizon and what sampling do bank-retreat forecasts need?** A skill-and-coverage-vs-horizon curve
   (h = 1, 3, 5) plus a thinning experiment (annual vs every 3rd/5th year vs the 4-9 dates typical of DSAS studies).
   Why: it uses the forecast pipeline as is, it generalises beyond the Mekong (any Landsat bank or shoreline study),
   and it directly supports H1/H2. Nearest work (Donovan et al. 2019 ESR; Donovan & Belmont 2019 ESPL; Jautzy et al.
   2020) treats measurement error and timescale bias, not forecast skill or interval coverage. Status: partly done
   (measurement side); the forecast side was not found. Exploratory: the 2019-2024 test period was already scored on
   26/10, so the thinning and horizon curves are post hoc; log them as a dated deviation, keep the h = 3/5 protocol
   results unchanged, and preferably show the thinning curve on the calibration years as well as the test years.
2. **C4 + C12: what official erosion reporting captures.** Length- and area-weighted completeness (share of measured
   retreat in reported units) and precision (share of reported bank length without measurable retreat), by river,
   channel type and exposure tercile, plus the lead-time skill of warned-reach lists against satellite persistence.
   Why: it turns the negative H3 result into a policy-relevant finding for NHESS (warning lists label 75% of units
   and are unrelated to measured retreat). Nearest work is in other hazards (Veh et al. 2022 GLOFs; Franceschini et
   al. 2022 landslides); not found for riverbank erosion. Must be labelled exploratory (the agreement table is seen).
3. **C2: island banks vs main-channel banks.** Retreat rates, persistence and head/tail pattern of the 11 islands
   measured all round, against Vu et al. 2024 (VMD islets net accretion by area). Status: partly done (area-based);
   bank-level annual all-round measurement not found. Exploratory. Best kept short in the NHESS paper or expanded for
   the Geomorphology fallback.

Do not pursue as separate contributions:
- C5 (built-up vs retreat as a protection signal): confounded without an embankment inventory and pushes toward
  causal claims; keep the existing sentence in the H3 discussion.
- C10 (episodic vs gradual from annual increments): annual changes are mostly below the noise floor at 30 m.
- C11 (hotspot persistence) and C6 (transferability): already covered by the H1 baselines and the protocol's
  leave-block-out design.
- C3 (regime shifts) as a separate result: overlaps Nguyen Dam Quoc et al. 2026 and is circular with H2's
  pre-declared change periods; describe only.
- C8 as a novelty claim: release the dataset (data availability, Zenodo) without a "first" claim, given Dente et al. 2026.
- C9 stays the opening figure already planned in the roadmap, not a contribution.
